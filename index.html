What the index measures

The index counts how many of six types of support a state or UT startup policy offers women founders specifically, over and above what is open to all startups. A state scores 1 for each type where the policy contains at least one women-specific provision, so the score runs from 0 to 6.

It measures breadth of coverage. It does not measure how large a scheme is, how many women use it, or whether it is paid out.

The six categories

Data and evidence

  • Policy provisions: coded from state startup policies, notifications and official state pages, with a research cut-off of 30 October 2026. Each Yes has a description of the provision and the eligibility attached to it.
  • Policy on record: for Gujarat, Jharkhand, Meghalaya, Delhi, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Mizoram and West Bengal, the provisions reflect the startup policy on record. Newer documents, if any, are not reflected. These states are tagged on their pages.
  • Startup counts: DPIIT-recognised startups and women-led startups from the Startup India state policy page, extracted 31 October 2026. Women-led means at least one woman co-founder, which is broader than majority women-owned. Five UTs (Chandigarh, Delhi, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Ladakh, Lakshadweep) are not reported there.

Reading a score of 0

A 0 means no women-specific provision was found in the policy reviewed. It does not mean the state offers no support to women. General startup benefits, and targets or definitions without an incentive, are not counted.

Boundaries on the map are for illustration only.
